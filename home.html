A LITTLE CLARITY

Before your beautiful beginning.

Can we create our own wedding package?

Absolutely. Choose your planning level, aesthetic, services and celebration scale in our package builder. Your estimate updates with every selection.

What do the prices include?

Prices are illustrative starting estimates in Indian rupees. The aesthetic covers design direction; décor covers execution. The experience fee covers guest coordination, not accommodation. Final vendor costs, travel and taxes are confirmed separately.

How do we take the next step?

Build a package, then open the contact page to prepare your enquiry. You can download it or send it through your email app.
